Fires N transfers at the same instant against one source account, exercising the full production engine: Mutex rate-limiter, OTP, and SELECT … FOR UPDATE row locks. Pick amount × tasks larger than the source balance to force contention — the locks serialize the balance checks, so the account can never be overdrawn.

More than 5 also demos the per-minute Mutex rate limit.
